I will be an invited speaker, joining online, at [LASAI 2026](https://simbig.org/lasai/2026/), the Latin American School of Artificial Intelligence, held from 23 to 26 October 2026 at the Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC) in Lima, Peru. The school brings together researchers and practitioners from across Latin America, with most of the programme delivered in Spanish, and combines in-person sessions with an online day.
