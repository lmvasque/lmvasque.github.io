Citation and BibTeX travel in a data-clip attribute, read by copy-cite.html on click. They cannot sit in an element's text: _layouts/default.html applies `layout: compress`, which strips newlines site-wide and would flatten BibTeX onto one line. Attribute values survive it, so newlines are encoded as and the browser decodes them back when parsing.
